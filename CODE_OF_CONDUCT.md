# Community Code of Conduct

myCloset aims to provide a welcoming, practical, and respectful collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the sensitivity of user-uploaded photos.
- Use inclusive language and avoid assumptions about gender, bodies, culture, or identity.
- Accept correction and help others learn.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information or images without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, credentials, or unsafe data handling.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or bad-faith engagement.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or restrict contributors when necessary. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
